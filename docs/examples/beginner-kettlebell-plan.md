# Beginner plan: 3 days of lifting (one 16 kg kettlebell, at home) + 3 easy runs

Goal: build muscle, plus 30 minutes of easy running 2 to 3 times a week.
Equipment: one 16 kg kettlebell, a chair or bench, floor space. Running at the gym or outside.

## Weekly schedule

Option 1: separate days (more recovery between sessions)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Day A (lift) |
| Tue | Run, 30 min easy |
| Wed | Day B (lift) |
| Thu | Run, 30 min easy |
| Fri | Day C (lift) |
| Sat | Run, 30 min easy (optional) |
| Sun | Rest |

Option 2: same day (lift and run together, 4 full rest days)

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Day A, then a 30 min easy run |
| Tue | Rest |
| Wed | Day B, then a 30 min easy run |
| Thu | Rest |
| Fri | Day C, then a 30 min easy run (optional) |
| Sat | Rest |
| Sun | Rest |

If you do both on the same day:
- Lift first, run after, or put 6 or more hours between them.
- Running first leaves your legs too tired for squats, swings and split squats.
- Keep the run easy. It should never be the hard part of the day.
- If you are wiped out, skip the run before you skip the lifting.

## Day A
Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets.

| Exercise | Sets x reps |
|---|---|
| Goblet squat | 3 x 12 to 15 |
| Kettlebell floor press (lie on the floor, one arm at a time) | 3 x 8 to 10 per arm |
| One-arm kettlebell row (free hand on a chair or bench) | 3 x 12 to 15 per side |
| Kettlebell swing | 4 x 12 to 15 |
| Plank | 3 x 30 to 45 sec |

## Day B

| Exercise | Sets x reps |
|---|---|
| Kettlebell Romanian deadlift (two hands) | 3 x 12 |
| Kettlebell overhead press (one arm) | 3 x 5 to 6 per arm |
| Push-up (on your knees if needed) | 3 x as many as you can |
| Reverse lunge (bell held at your chest) | 3 x 8 to 10 per leg |
| Suitcase carry (walk with the bell in one hand) | 3 x 30 sec per side |

If you cannot press the bell for 5 reps with good form, do the floor press and push-ups for a few
weeks, and keep trying the overhead press at the end of the session for 2 to 3 reps.

## Day C

| Exercise | Sets x reps |
|---|---|
| Bulgarian split squat (back foot on a chair, bell at your chest) | 3 x 8 per leg |
| Kettlebell swing | 4 x 12 to 15 |
| One-arm kettlebell row | 3 x 12 to 15 per side |
| Single-leg glute bridge (bell on your hips when it gets easy) | 3 x 12 per leg |
| Halo + dead bug | 2 x 10 each |

## Running
- Run easy, at a pace where you can still speak in full sentences. Most beginners go too fast.
- If 30 minutes straight is too much: alternate 2 minutes of running with 1 minute of walking for
  the full 30. Shorten the walking breaks a little each week until you run the whole time.
- Do not run hard the day before a leg session.

## How to progress (one fixed weight, so make each rep harder)
Work down this list for each exercise:
1. Add reps until you reach about 15 to 20 per set.
2. Slow the lowering (3 seconds down).
3. Pause for 1 to 2 seconds at the hardest point of the lift.
4. Switch to a single-leg or one-arm version.

When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, move to the next step.

Move up when:

| Exercise | Move up when |
|---|---|
| Kettlebell swing | 5 x 20, then try one-arm swings |
| Goblet squat | You reach 20 reps. Move to split squats in 3 to 5 weeks |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 x 12 per leg, then slow the lowering to 3 seconds |
| Floor press | 3 x 12 to 15 per arm, then add a pause and a slow lowering |
| Overhead press | 3 x 8 to 10 per arm |
| One-arm row | Slow lowering (3 seconds) and a 2-second pause at the top |
| Romanian deadlift | Move to one leg, 3 x 8 per leg |
| Single-leg glute bridge | 3 x 15 to 20, then add the bell on your hips and pause at the top |
| Suitcase carry | 3 x 45 to 60 sec per side |
| Push-up | Add reps, then slow the lowering |

## What gets outgrown first
1. Legs: goblet squats and two-handed Romanian deadlifts get light first. Move to single-leg
   versions as soon as the reps feel easy.
2. Back: the row gets light next. Use slow lowering and holds.
3. Chest: push-ups and the floor press last longest.
4. Shoulders: the overhead press may be tough at first.

Legs are the weak spot of this setup. If you keep training for muscle, the best next buy is a heavier
second bell (around 24 kg) or adjustable dumbbells. Wait until you can do 12 to 15 reps per leg on
split squats.

## Other notes
- First two weeks: go lighter on the reps and learn the movements. Film yourself on the squat,
  swing and Romanian deadlift to check form.
- Swing: it is a hip hinge, not a squat. The bell floats up from your hips, not your arms.
- Deload: take an easier week every 6 to 8 weeks (about 60% of the usual reps and sets).
- Sleep and protein matter more than any exercise choice for building muscle.
